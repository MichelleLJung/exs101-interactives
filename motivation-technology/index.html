EXS101 · Exercise Psychology

Motivation & Technology

Use your notes from seven days of app use to explain how one feature affected your behavior. Complete the report, preview it, and save a PDF for Canvas.

Save a draft file to finish later. Save a PDF from the report preview to submit to Canvas. Your entries and evidence are processed in this browser.

Starting point

Seven-day record

Use the calendar to select the date for each of your seven entries. The dates may be nonconsecutive. Record what you noticed about the feature and how you responded. A day counts if you used the app, even if you skipped the activity.

Example. Feature: The app reminded me to walk during class. Response: I dismissed the reminder and skipped the walk.

Proof of use

Upload one screenshot from your observation period showing use, such as a dated entry, progress history, or a recorded interaction with the feature. Crop or cover private details before uploading.

Use this option if your app blocks screenshots or an image cannot be shared safely. Paste the relevant part of an export showing actual use during your observation period.

Interpretation

Choose two dates from your seven-day record

Pick two entries that help you explain how you responded to the app feature. They can show the same pattern or different responses.

Each day unlocks after you enter its date, what you noticed, and how you responded.

Recommendation

Choose Save as PDF in the print dialog. Open the saved file to check it, then upload it to Canvas.
