EXS101 Fitness Assessment Lab

Measure. Compare. Interpret.

Physical fitness includes multiple characteristics, and no single assessment can tell us everything about a person. Perform field assessments, record your actual results, compare appropriate results with reference data, and evaluate what each measurement can—and cannot—tell you.
