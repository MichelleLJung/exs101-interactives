Estimate, Not Measurement

Compendium MET values are standardized estimates. This calculation does not directly measure an individual's actual energy expenditure. Individual physiology, movement efficiency, fitness, environmental conditions, and other factors can affect actual expenditure. The total includes the resting component during this time.

Activity values: 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities (ages 19–59). 1 MET is the standard resting reference, not a measurement of this person's resting metabolism.
