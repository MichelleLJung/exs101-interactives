About the estimate

The simplified 4/4/9 factors estimate food energy from carbohydrate, protein, and fat. Actual food-label totals can differ because of rounding, fiber, sugar alcohols, and other calculation methods. This model excludes alcohol. kcal values are not ATP amounts or exercise fuel-use percentages.
