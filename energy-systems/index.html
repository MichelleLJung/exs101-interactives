Why does the pattern change? The systems differ in how rapidly they can regenerate ATP and how much ATP they can supply over time. Phosphagen can respond extremely rapidly but has limited capacity; oxidative metabolism has very large capacity but a lower maximal rate. The glycolytic system helps meet high ATP demand between those extremes.

About This Model

This is a conceptual learning model, not an individual metabolic prediction. Actual contributions vary with exercise mode, pacing, training status, and measurement method. Use it to reason about patterns—not to assign exact percentages to a person or sport.
