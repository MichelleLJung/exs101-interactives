4.9 L/min

70 beats/min × 70 mL/beat = 4,900 mL/min

What does that number mean?

At 70 beats per minute and 70 mL per beat, the heart pumps approximately 4.9 liters of blood each minute.

Cardiac Output = Heart Rate × Stroke Volume

During dynamic exercise, heart rate and stroke volume typically increase from rest. Together, these changes increase cardiac output so the heart can pump more blood each minute.
