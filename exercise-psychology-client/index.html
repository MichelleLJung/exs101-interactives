EXERCISE PSYCHOLOGY

Simulated Client Investigation

Investigate · Interpret · Recommend · Reassess

Your challenge

Act as a team of exercise science professionals. Discover why your client struggles to adopt or maintain physical activity, explain their experiences using course concepts, and test a realistic recommendation. Your goal is understanding, not simply convincing someone to exercise.

By the end: Explain three concepts using client evidence, propose and revise a strategy, and connect your findings to a behavior outside exercise.

How to complete the investigation (about 40 minutes)
  1. Investigate (10 min): Interview your client before giving advice.
  2. Interpret (8 min): Use the reference pages to explain three relevant concepts.
  3. Recommend (5 min): Agree on a realistic strategy and why it fits.
  4. Reassess (7 min): Present it to your client, ask for feedback and revise.
  5. Debrief (10 min): Compare with another group and transfer the concepts beyond exercise.

How the conversation limit works

Each interview allows up to 30 successful messages from your group. This is a maximum, not a target. Save some messages for testing your recommendation. When the limit is reached, or the 90-minute session expires, live replies stop; your conversation and notes remain on screen. Finish the discussion without sending more messages.
